    Welcome to Cub Scout Pack 66 Kingston: Adventure, Learning, and Community for Families

    Discover the Joy of Cub Scouting in
    South County

    At Cub Scout Pack 66, based in South Kingstown, RI, we provide an engaging and family-friendly environment for children in kindergarten through fifth grade to explore, learn, and grow. Our Pack welcomes families to help create a vibrant community where kids experience the excitement of outdoor adventures, skill-building activities, and team camaraderie. We strive to make scouting activities accessible to all.

    Why Pack 66 Stands Out: Expertise and Community Commitment

    Cub Scout Pack 66 is more than just a scouting group; we are a dedicated team of experienced leaders and enthusiastic families committed to nurturing the potential of every child. Our Pack emphasizes safety, inclusivity, and respect, ensuring a supportive environment where children develop leadership skills, confidence, and lifelong friendships. Join Pack 66 to be part of a community that values growth, fun, and family involvement in the heart of South Kingstown.

    Comprehensive Resources for Current Families

    For our current Pack 66 families, our website serves as a convenient hub for accessing important resources such as event calendars, registration forms, and announcements. We understand the busy lives of parents and guardians, so we keep everything organized and easy to find, helping families stay connected and involved in Pack activities. Whether you're a leader or a parent, our site empowers you to participate fully and make the most of your Cub Scout experience.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is Cub Scout Pack 66 Kingston?

    Pack 66 Kingston is a family-friendly Cub Scout pack serving the communities of Kingston, Wakefield, Peace Dale, and Matunuck in South Kingstown, RI and beyond. We provide youth development programs for children in Kindergarten through 5th grade including boys and girls.

    What are the meeting times and locations?

    Pack 66 meets weekly on Wednesday evenings at 6:30pm. During the warmer months we meet at Camp Aquapaug off Ministerial rd. Please check our calendar on the website for exact times and any schedule changes.

    Are there costs associated with joining Pack 66?

    Yes, there is an annual membership fee that covers national Cub Scout registration and Council fees. There are additional costs to cover uniforms, special events or camping trips.

    Where can I find the Pack 66 calendar?

    Our calendar is available on the website and is regularly updated with meeting dates, events, and important deadlines to keep families informed.

    How does Pack 66 communicate with families?

    We communicate through email, our website announcements and the Google Calendar to keep everyone informed and connected.

    How can parents get involved?

    Parents and guardians are encouraged to volunteer as leaders, event helpers, or committee members. We value family involvement to enrich the Pack experience for all Scouts. Email us to learn how you can become an adult leader in Scouting.
